main
- Hand-ins go to the NPC you talked to, never to an object you already used (Coruscant Bugged Out: Tookreek, not the Sealed Case).
- Mission givers are never a mob you fought or looted, or something you clicked long before. Missions you got by walking in or in a holocom call: the bot walks to where you got them.
- Profile comments and fight names are short step labels only.
- Walking out through a door runs while you are still inside (Black Hole Glow).
- Objects are clicked from where you stood. Pick-ups from objects are pinned to that exact object.
- A task's last count is kept when the mission finished on it (Section X Research Stations 3/3).
- Staged bonus missions wait for the stage before them. The Field Generator is walked up to and clicked before its boss fight.
- An object that starts waves is clicked once, not again and again (End of Torvix).
- PvP missions are left out of profiles.
- A profile with anything plain BuddyCron can't run is named "(not finished)" and says so when it starts.
- Recording starts only when you press Start recording. It no longer starts by itself when BuddyCron opens.
