Quest Recorder patch notes - LethToxic

1.2
The profile now copies what you did, in your order.
- Steps come in the order you did them, each at the exact spot you used. BuddyCron finds the way in between
  (no walk points to stop at).
- Fights: each one at the spots where you fought it, only the mobs whose kill counted for that task. Nothing from
  elsewhere in the recording. Fights sit at the top of the profile (GrindAreas) and the steps use them by name.
- Clicks: only the exact object you clicked. When two of the same kind stand close together (CZ-198 pods), the
  bot stands where you stood first, so it takes the same one you did.
- Selecting an object and attacking it counts as using that object.
- A lift you opened but walked past: the bot walks past it too instead of getting stuck at it.
- A count you stopped short of only runs on to the full count for plain fights (no waiting forever at a used pod).
- A new recording never joins onto a profile you already saved from the review list.
- A task's last count is written as its count (35 of 35), not just "step done".
- Containers that respawn in the same spot count as new ones, so every click you made is in the profile.
- Casts are recorded from the moment they start, so a step's cause comes before its tick, and a cast isn't lost
  when a fight starts. A loot that finishes just after the tick stays with that step.
- Your target is recorded every time it changes, and looting a dead mob is a loot, not "talked to an NPC".
- Quests that finish by themselves aren't "handed in" to whatever you clicked last.
- Lifts are left to BuddyCron, which rides them itself.
- The profile ends with "Done" (or tells you what isn't finished) instead of starting itself over.
- Distances fixed everywhere: game positions are in 10 m units, and about 20 checks treated them as metres.

- Mission items, exactly as you used them: where you stood, what you used it on (yourself, an NPC by name, a
  spot), your exact distance (the bot uses it from there - no more "TooFar"), and every use in your order: the last
  one, items with no cast bar, stims and injectors. Medpacs and items the game takes off you are left out. If the
  recorder can't tell, the step is yellow - it never guesses or drops a use.
- An item used on a corpse is used on dead ones (DeadOnly). An item used in a fight on a weakened mob: the bot
  fights it first and uses the item once its health is down to where you did.
- Several items in a row (runes): each one in your order, on the NPC you used it on, from as close as you stood.
- Scanner: while you record, the recorder asks the game what each mission counts and saves every one of those
  objects and mobs near you, also the ones you only walked past. Only what your missions still need.
- Collect steps (a counter fed by eggs, bones, and loot): one step until it's full. It takes any of those objects
  within 150 m, with one spot per cluster so the bot's loop is short.
- Fights also get the spots of linked groups of the same mobs near where you fought (within ~150 m), so the bot
  moves on to the next group instead of waiting for a respawn.
- Rewards: the reward is taken when the game says it's waiting, even if the quest already counts as done.
- Quest abilities from a special / temporary bar (Macrobinoculars, the Seeker Droid): recorded like mission items
  (where, on whom, how close, in your order) and cast by the new UseQuestAbility tag, which opens the bar with its
  mission item first. Only kept when a mission moved right after, so a new class ability never becomes a step.
- Putting on a quest item (a disguise): its own step (EquipItem), right before the step it was for. A gear swap
  with no mission progress after it is left out.
- Defend / hold a spot: when you held a spot while mobs came at you and the step moved on by itself, the bot waits
  there, fighting, until it moves on (instead of walking there and leaving).
- Mission items the game gives no sign for (no cast bar, no cooldown, nothing leaves your bags - the Serum Injector):
  the recorder also reads the game's own combat log, which names the item and who you used it on. Turn on
  "Enable Combat Logging" in the game's Preferences (Combat Logging) for this.
- A loot with no corpse click right after a kill (the game's area loot) counts as looting that mob, and a step
  finished by looting a mob you killed earlier becomes "kill and loot" it.
- An item the game takes when a mission finishes (handed in) is never guessed as used on someone.
- Clicks on side-by-side objects go by the exact spot you clicked, in your order (new ClickObject tag).
- A quick travel or fleet pass you cast never becomes walk points.
- Picking up a quest waits for the quest board to open. The summary waits for the save. The review list won't
  save over a profile that was changed outside it.

1.1b
- 1.1a's pick-up was broken: the bot walked to the giver and didn't pick anything up. Fixed, the Recon Droid in
  Black Hole included.

1.1a
- Updates now come straight from the plugin's source on GitHub (no more zip files): the plugin sits at the top of
  the repository, the way BuddyCron plugins usually do. You can also clone or download it into BuddyCron\Plugins\QuestRecorder.
- NPC quest windows (the Recon Droid's Asset Liquidation) are accepted by the bot, using BuddyCron's new quest window
  support (update BuddyCron to its latest build).

1.1
Recording - everything you do, nothing guessed:
- "What you did (live)": every click, item, reply, window, board and lift shows up as you play, with what it did
  in your quest log. OK = the bot does it by itself. !! = it can't, the line says why, and it pops up in game.
- Each recording also saves "(what you did).txt": every action, exactly where, and what changed in your quest log.
- The quest giver is whatever you clicked right before it landed in your log (General Konya for End of Torvix, the
  Recon Droid for Asset Liquidation, the terminal for the dailies).
- Steps are the clicks you made, in your order, at the exact spots - no more guessing objects from their names.
- The interact key counts as a use, and every key or mouse action over an object is recorded.
- Conversations: the reply you pick is recorded. Light or dark picks use TempConvoSkip; other picks use the new
  TalkAndReply step, which picks the same wheel numbers you did.
- Mission items: caught by cast, cooldown, a stack being used up, quest items in your bags, and "Using X..." casts.
  Several used one after another (the Sith runes) are each used once, in your order.
- Scanner: when you click a quest object, every other one with the same name close by is noted too.
- Hotkeys while recording: Ctrl+Shift+P = pause here (you do it by hand), Ctrl+Shift+W = wait here 10 s,
  Ctrl+Shift+Z = ignore your last action.

After you stop - you're in charge:
- Review list: your profile step by step in plain words. Untick a step to leave it out, move it up or down, or
  mark it "do by hand", then Save. No XML. Steps with no proof (nothing you did was seen) are yellow.

Better profiles:
- Fights roam every spot you killed at; one kill counting for two quests keeps both fights going; a task you
  stopped short of still runs to the full count.
- "Click any of these" is one step with every spot, so the bot uses whichever one is there.
- Puzzles (6+ clicks on the same object in one step, like the End of Torvix pipeline controls) become a
  "do it by hand" pause on that exact object.
- The thing you clicked right before a boss fight (Kregg's Locker) is clicked again first.
- Pick-ups give up after 3 tries if the giver isn't offering the quest (no more clicking a terminal forever), and
  handle mission boards and conversations by themselves. An NPC's own quest window (the Recon Droid) still needs
  you to press Accept - the profile asks you.
- Heroic shuttles: taken from the Fleet too, and when you started recording mid-cast.

Also in 1.1:
- Bot stuck? If the bot sits on one step for 5 minutes and nothing in your quest log moves, a pop-up
  offers to open the fix panel right at that step.
- Record from here: in the control panel, pick a step, press Record from here and play just the bit
  the bot got wrong. It goes into the profile by itself - no re-recording the whole planet.
- Carry on: logged off halfway through a planet? Your next recording there (within a day) carries on
  in the same profile instead of starting a new one.
- Send report: one button packs your recording, the profile and your BuddyCron log into a zip (your
  character name is taken out). Drop it in Discord and say what went wrong.
- Double-click a line in the task tracker and the control panel opens right at that step.
- Control panel is much simpler: big buttons for the usual fixes (Click this, Talk to this, Fight these,
  Use my mission item, Walk here, Wait, Remove, Undo). It works out the quest step by itself. The full
  list is still there under More options.
- More options: Replace step (swap a step for a new one), Check my profile (spots steps that
  will probably go wrong) and Share (packs the profile so you can post it).
- The main window is simpler: settings moved to their own Settings button, the task tracker gets the space.
- First time you open it, a short how-it-works.
- When you stop recording it tells you where your profile is.

1.0g
- Heroic shuttles: short hops are taken too. A shuttle counts as needed when you're further than half the hop
  you made (between 15 and 100 m) - Saving Face (a 50 m hop) used to get walked.

1.0f
- Mission pickups are back to the 1.0b check: dailies and heroics only if they're not in your log and not done,
  weeklies only if they're not in your log. Rebuild profiles made with 1.0c-1.0e ("Make profile from a recording").

1.0e ("Make profile from a recording").

1.0e
- Fixed: 1.0c and 1.0d left the fights out of new profiles. Please rebuild any profile you made with them
  ("Make profile from a recording").
- Mission items used several times for one task (like the Radiation Sensor for the spies) are used once per count.
- A guessed object has to be near where the quest moved on (no more walking off to a same-named thing elsewhere).
- The game's hidden helper quests don't add walk steps any more.

1.0d
- Fixed the bot standing still at the wrong object: when the recorder couldn't see what you did for a quest
  (like identifying spies in the Black Hole), it sometimes picked an object you only used later. Now it only
  ever picks something you'd already used at that point.

1.0c
- Fixed BuddyCron crashing when loading some recorded profiles (a recording that ended on an elevator, or
  a quest you got without a giver, left an empty step in the profile).
- Weeklies, dailies and heroics get picked up again whenever the game offers them (weeklies up to 3 times
  a week), and the profile doesn't loop forever on a weekly.
- Shorter wait after a Heroic Transport (5 seconds instead of 10).

1.0b
- Runs smoother: profiles no longer have a walk point every few metres - BuddyCron's navigator does the walking.
  They only keep what matters: clicks, talks, fights, doors, lifts, shuttles, rewards.
- Heroic shuttle steps no longer pause for 10 seconds when there's no shuttle to use.

1.0a
- Checks for updates as soon as BuddyCron starts, even if Quest Recorder isn't ticked.
- The update pop-up now shows on top of the game instead of hiding behind it.

1.0
First proper release. Play it once, get the profile.
- Records everything you do by hand and turns it into a profile that does it the same way.
- Heroic Transport shuttles, mission items, elevators, puzzles, boss terminals, NPC clicks,
  looting bodies, rewards (weekly included).
- Live task tracker with "!!" when something was missed, plus an in-game heads-up.
- Control panel: fix or build a profile without touching the XML. It only offers what BuddyCron doesn't
  already do by itself (taxis, elevators, quick travel, vendoring and looting are automatic).
- Make a profile from any old recording.
- Update pop-up, so you never have to download it again by hand.
- "Recorded by <your name>" credit if you want it.
- Ctrl+Shift+Z undo, Ctrl+Shift+M marker.

Coming from the beta (3.x)? Install 1.0 once by hand or with Install Quest Recorder.bat -
the beta's updater won't see it. Updates are automatic from here on.
