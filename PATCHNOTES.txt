Quest Recorder patch notes - LethToxic

1.6
- Binocular scans leave the view once the scan counts (the hand-fixed Yavin 4 one): the bot reads the mission's step
  and counts when it starts the scan, and as soon as they move on it lets go of the zoom and closes the view, checked
  by the game (Escape on the view's own controls, then the Escape key while the game is in front). After 15 s with no
  count it leaves the view too, and a fight or a death closes it first.
- Rest to full before walking to objects to click, and between clicks after a fight (the hand-fixed Tatooine one): out
  of a fight, under 95% health or the companion under 90%, the bot uses the class rest (up to 20 s). The combat routine
  only rests below 77%, so a walk into the next camp at 80% pulled the next group with a fifth of the health gone.
- A step that only needs you to get somewhere waits there, and walks a few steps back the way you came and on to it
  again, until the game counts it (the hand-fixed Voss one: Locate the Vile Grotto, Locate the Tomb). About a minute at
  most, then the profile goes on. Stages the game passes through by itself still just wait for the next step.
- The end of the profile waits for a reward window only when one can come (a mission done and not handed in, or its
  window open), not every run.

1.5
- Objects to use are clicked by one routine everywhere, the one from the hand-made Voss and Tatooine profiles: it
  clicks the nearest object of the name that the game still lets you use, from where you stood for it, one at a time,
  and counts a click only when the game's own count goes up. It never walks a round of spots (BuddyCron's UseObject
  with spots ran between Spice Crates for minutes), lets BuddyCron fight, revive and talk first, and leaves an object it
  can't reach. Spot-to-spot UseObject is left only where a count is mostly kills (1 step in 50 recordings, was 71).
- A bonus you didn't do is not done at all, even when your kills counted for it: no fight, no pick-up ask, not in the
  end check (Tatooine's Thunderstruck, Jedi Rescuers, Exterminate the Exchange, Exchange of Violence). Its reward is
  only taken if the game offers it.
- An enemy you clicked to target and then killed is never the step's cause (Voss: the Commando Captain took the pyre's
  step).
- Mission transports are pressed by the game's item id for that mission, from the game's quest data (306 missions):
  Korriban's, Tython's and Voss's heroics each give one called "Heroic Transport", and by name the bot took the first
  one it found. With each mission's own transport known, the "again" rounds for same-named transports are gone.
- A transport is used while the mission is on any step you did at its site, not only the first: a bot started again
  mid-mission, or sent off by a death, gets back there.
- A door you clicked to go through (Voss's Trial of the First Chamber Door) is clicked again when the bot is outside it
  later in the mission, then it waits until it is through.
- Every hook gives way to a conversation (BuddyCron answers it first): object clicks, transports, loot, item uses.
- Checks at the top of "(what you did).txt": what keeps the profile from running, a fight for a bonus you didn't do,
  spot-to-spot object use, a transport known only by a shared name, and each step the maker wasn't sure of. A Hook put
  in and never taken out now marks the profile not finished.

1.4c
- A bonus you finished is done after its mission's steps, not before them: the steps went by when they started, and a
  bonus count starts with your first kill (Tatooine's gunners were ground before the consoles, the Exchange forces
  before the Spice Crates, the Jedi Rescuers before the prisoners). When the mission's end would take the bonus away,
  the mission's last step is split: all but one, the bonus, then the last one.
- A bonus you never finished is not hunted (Jawa Trade: 11 of 18 Jawas; Black Box: 5 of 12 Sand People, where the bot
  waited for respawns). Only missions the game names a bonus: hidden helper missions keep their steps.
- Clickable objects are searched for only as far as the gap to the next one, and never more than 10 m from one you
  used: with two Spice Crates the same distance away the bot kept switching between them, and on Hutta it took the lift
  down to a Fabrication Unit on the floor below instead of the one you used.
- A step that changed well after a loot and away from it (more than 4 s and 20 m) is a walk to where it changed, not a
  fight at the body: Hostage Situation Cleanup's "Get to a Safe Distance" had the bot standing at Baranj's body.
- A mission transport works from anywhere: started on a planet or ship the recording never went to, with the mission in
  your log, the bot presses it where it stands (BuddyCron's own UseItem walks to where you pressed it first, and that is
  in another area). Out of a fight, a cooldown waited out, 3 tries at most.
- A walk to where a step ticked goes to where you landed when it ticked on a lift or in a jump (Hutta's "Get to the
  Basement" ticked 3 m above the floor, and BuddyCron's walk never ended).
- Mission transports are pressed where the bot stands everywhere, out of a fight, and the step is done once you are at
  the mission's site (or in another area). BuddyCron's UseItem was given where you pressed it, and it kills every enemy
  near that spot before it presses: you mostly pressed it where the mission before ended, so with that mission done
  the bot fought its enemies again (Tatooine: Jedi Rescuers before Supply Lines).
- Several objects to use: the ones you used are used first, in your order, each from where you stood, and only if it
  can still be used; BuddyCron's UseObject does what is left. It also takes any object of that name near the bot, and a
  Spice Crate a step up from the one it headed for pulled it back and forth for minutes.
- Binoculars: the view opens where the camera looks and only the mouse turns it (Yavin 4: no key moved it). The bot
  now aims it with small mouse moves, each checked by reading where the camera looks, and only while the game is the
  window in front. It zooms with the binoculars' own zoom key held down (found even when BuddyCron's list of the
  game's controls misses it), then the mouse wheel, then the right button. One try, then the pop-up (it tried for
  2 minutes). Held buttons are let go after a fight, a death or 20 s.

1.4b
- Binoculars: when you scan with binoculars, the recorder saves where the camera was looking (left and right, and up
  and down) and what you scanned. The profile turns and aims the camera the same way before it scans. Only binocular
  scans are aimed; nothing else changes.
- Transports: when several transports go on cooldown at the same moment, the last one is the one you pressed (Hutta's
  Steel Voice transport was read as Factory Recall's).
- Your own skills from the combat log are mission steps only when a mission item, a mission's special bar or a
  mission's objective names them (Hutta's Holotraverse and Stealth were steps, and two talk steps were lost).
- A step is named from the branch you took (Steel Voice's dark choice: "Apply Chemicals to the Filter Station").
- Starting a heroic profile again partway: the bot knows it's already at a mission's site when it stands where you did
  that site's other missions or bonuses. A transport with more than 5 minutes of cooldown left is skipped instead of
  pressed forever.
- A click on an NPC is a step only if it opened a conversation or made the mission move (Tatooine's Sand People
  Clansman and Exchange Sentinel clicks were "talk to" steps).
- A bar that ends as a mission moves is a use of an object when you stand next to an object one of your missions
  counts right after a loot, or your mouse was on that object as the bar began (Tatooine's black box was read as
  looting a Sand People Savage).
- A kill that finishes a mission on the spot is its own fight (War on Spice's bonus boss Sindal Reed was missing).
- A pop-up that asks you to do a binocular scan or a puzzle yourself has a working Continue button (BuddyCron greys it
  out unless the profile turns it on, so you could only stop the bot).
- "What you did": a binocular scan shows the mission step it did. The list looked only 10 s ahead and a scan can take
  longer (Yavin 4's Chopping Block scan took 11 s and read as "nothing").
- Binoculars: the profile turns, tilts and zooms the view with the binocular view's own keys first, then the camera's
  and the mouse's, and only on the input layers the game has on while the view is open (on Yavin 4 the movement and
  game layers are off then). It writes which keys it tried to BuddyCron's log as "[Binoculars]" lines.
- A transport is pressed where you pressed it. A step with no spot made BuddyCron walk to <0, 0, 0> first (Tatooine's
  heroics never left the fleet).
- Two missions done at the same moment (a heroic's last boss and the weekly it counts for): the boss stays the heroic's
  own step (Voss's Suvar-Ton and Guardian of Dark History, Nar Shaddaa's Vrblther Hive Guardian and CZ-198's second
  Anti-Toxin were missing).
- A medpac or adrenal you used in fights is never a mission step, even when the boss died right after it (Voss).
  "What you did" says so on its line too (it said a medpac finished Voss's Ancient Guardians).
- "What you did" shows a mission that ended right after a fight. It was left out (Voss's Ancient Guardians and the
  weekly, the CZ-198 and Section X dailies, the Black Hole's Control the Battlefield).
- A transport belongs to the mission you went for, not to a bonus the game still hid when you pressed it (Tython's
  Speechless comes with The Chamber of Speech at the board but shows only at the site: the bot might never have pressed
  the transport).
- A bar that opens a conversation with a device, and the mission moves as that talk ends, is a use of the device under
  your mouse, also without the combat log (Tython's Ancient Computer was read as a fight with the Lore Guardian).
  "What you did" names the device you talked to.
- The wait for a reward is timed from the end of the mission's last step, not its start (some waits were minutes long).
- A walk through a door or phase runs only in the zone of that door (Voss-Ka's door walk could run on the fleet).
- Your own skills (a heal-up like Channel Hatred, your mount) are never clicks on objects, even when you had just clicked
  an object (Voss: every heal-up became a mission click without the combat log). New recordings mark them.
- A step done by a lift ride and then a walk: the bot walks on to where the mission moved on (Black Hole's Glow ended
  at the terminal, past the lift).
- A mission whose last step is done by walking somewhere (Coruscant's Trouble in Deed: "Leave the Shop") gets that walk
  before its reward. It was missing, so the bot would have stopped at the vault with "A quest from this profile isn't
  finished". A weekly that ends at the same moment takes its reward after that mission's last step too (without the
  combat log, Voss's two weekly rewards were waited for before their last boss fights).
- A step with no count of its own is not named after another task that has its own step (Republic's Most Wanted's
  Burnout fight was called "Defeat Chopper (Burnout)"; it is now "Kill Burnout").
- "What you did" shows every mission that ended with nothing you did just before it: "you got there" when you had
  walked on to that spot, else "it ended by itself" (Trouble in Deed and the Coruscant weekly were left out).
- "What you did": an item that left your bags is never what moved a mission (it said the transport the game took as
  Republic's Most Wanted ended had completed it; the line for the Burnout fight now says so). A mission you got that
  no other line gave has its own line, with the board or NPC the recorder saw, or "no giver seen" (Voss's Protected
  Writings, and missions taken one by one from a board you kept open).
- A mission board is never the giver of a mission the game gave you in another zone (Ord Mantell's Savrip Skullcaver
  came as you landed off the transport; the profile also had a step to take it from the fleet board).
- A talk that moved nothing, followed right away by a talk with the same NPC that moved the mission, doesn't decide the
  side the bot answers by (Ord Mantell's DK-9: you picked "Destroy it" and left, then talked again and took "I'll do
  it"; the profile was set to light side, so the bot would have destroyed the shipment). "What you did" says so on
  those reply lines.
- "What you did": a click on your companion is never what moved a mission (Ord Mantell: a Savrip Chieftain kill read
  as the click on your companion).
- An object that opens a conversation when you use it is used even though the game doesn't tie it to the mission
  (Coruscant's Sealed Case: the bot stood next to it and never clicked it). The talk is what moves the mission, so the
  step now tells BuddyCron not to wait for that tie, like hand-made profiles do (Hutta's Cantina Holoterminal too).
- Kill and loot with full bags: BuddyCron opens no bodies at all when your bags have 1 free slot or less, but a
  mission item goes in the mission bag and needs no room (Tython's Power Cells: the bot fought the Sentinels and never
  looted one). A fight whose count comes from looting now opens that mission's own bodies itself when your bags are
  full, out of a fight, and takes what fits. With room in your bags BuddyCron loots as before.
- A mission board you stood very close to: the bot walks to where you stood, not to the board's own spot, which it
  can't stand in, so the walk never ended (Voss's Heroic Missions board; the Black Hole, Section X, CZ-198 and Nar
  Shaddaa terminals had the same walk).

1.4a
- Every step runs only in the area you did it in: a mission board pick-up only on the fleet, a planet's step only on
  that planet (the bot no longer walks to a spot of another map).
- Missions whose transports share a name (Korriban's two "Heroic Transport"s): the bot uses the transport until it stands
  where that mission's steps are, never does one mission's steps while it stands at the other's, and goes round once
  more for the mission the transport didn't take it to first.
- A transport is tied to the mission its name holds (Tatooine's Call Down the Thunder was tied to War on Spice when the
  weekly reset took them both at once).
- A transport from the fleet with a long loading screen is found (Tatooine's Prison Labor transport was missing, so the
  bot never left the fleet).
- Where a transport put you is checked with height too (Nar Shaddaa's heroics sit floor above floor).
- A step whose kills the game counts in hidden parts is one fight with every kind you killed for it (Korriban's
  "Slay the K'lor'slugs": all four slugs, not one fight per slug).
- Two objects of one name at one spot (Korriban's two Red Engines): the bot takes the usable one and doesn't need to see
  it first. A container that came back after you used it is not a second object.
- A bonus mission the game ends when its heroic or daily is done (Tatooine's Crash Cleanup at 5 of 12, Section X's
  Prison Break): the bot does as many as you did, not the full count, and doesn't go to the board to take it on its own
  (Tatooine's A Shot at Bloodgouge comes with Blood and Sand).
- A mission's reward never comes before its last step when both happened at the same moment (Tatooine's Black Box
  ambush), and steps of the same moment keep the order you did them in.
- "What you did" no longer lists transport presses you never made: an item the game took away says so, the transport
  seen again as you landed isn't listed, and items that share a cooldown with the one you pressed say so.

1.4
- Profiles are made a new way: each mission step is tied to what you did for it by the game's own ids for that step and
  by the game's combat log, and each step is written with BuddyCron's own steps (pick up, fight, click, use an item,
  talk, hand in, walk), like a hand-made profile. Still plain BuddyCron, no plugin needed to run it.
- Turn on "Enable Combat Logging" in the game's Preferences (Combat Logging) before you record. Quest Recorder says so
  when you press Start recording and the game isn't writing a combat log.
- After Stop recording (and after Make profile), the Quest Recorder window says how the steps were found: by the game's
  own ids, by the one action you did, and how many need a look. The ones that need a look are yellow in the review list.
- The task tracker and the review list show the profile's own steps, with the same numbers as the profile.
- Something you did for a step before the action that moved it on is done once, not again and again.
- A medpac or stim you used in a fight is never a mission step.
- A step you finished by getting somewhere: the bot walks there. A step the game moves on by itself a few seconds later:
  the bot waits a moment.
- Hand-ins go to the NPC you talked to, never to an object you had already used for a step (Coruscant Bugged Out: the bot
  kept clicking the empty Sealed Case instead of handing in to Tookreek).
- Mission givers are only what really gave you the mission: never a mob you fought or looted, never something you clicked
  a while before the conversation that gave it, never something far from where you got it, and never an object that only
  brought out a fight. Missions you got by walking in or in a holocom call: the bot walks to where you got them.
- A talk step is only written for the NPC you were still talking to, or the one you clicked and stayed next to. A body you
  looted is never a talk.
- Taxi, lift, vendor and mission board names come from what you stood at, not from something you clicked a few seconds before.
- Your own abilities (Recuperate, a mount) no longer count as using the object next to you.
- Profile comments and fight names are short step labels. How a step was found is shown in the Quest Recorder window only.
- Walking out through a door runs while you are still inside (Black Hole Glow only walked out once it was already outside).
- Objects are clicked from where you stood, not from on top of them. Pick-ups from objects are pinned to that exact object
  (a twin terminal next to it is never taken). The weekly from the terminal walks to the same spot.
- A task's last count is in the profile when the mission finished on it (Section X Research Stations: 3 of 3, not 2 of 3).
- Staged bonus missions wait for the stage before them in the English client (Section X Research Cancellation: the
  Final Stage fight waits until the Research Stations are done).
- An object that brings out a boss or waves is walked up to and clicked once before the fight (Section X Field Generator,
  End of Torvix Master Safety Control), not clicked again and again while you fight. Also when you used or attacked it in
  the middle of the fight.
- Quest abilities from a special bar are used where you used them.
- A mission item used on NPCs that move around (Black Hole spies) is tried on every one of them, at every spot you used it.
- PvP missions (warzones, starfighter) are left out of profiles.
- A talk where you picked other replies than the bot would: the bot talks to that NPC and picks your replies.
- If anything in a profile can't run on plain BuddyCron, the profile is named "(not finished)", says so when it starts, and
  the Quest Recorder window says so too.
- Recording starts only when you press Start recording. It no longer starts by itself when BuddyCron opens.
- A pick-up or hand-in no longer gets an extra talk step in front of it.
- A mission that ends in a holocom call: the bot answers the call. A step that moved during a long story talk or call is
  done with that talk or call.
- A boss that talks once beaten: the fight finishes the step, not a talk to the boss.
- Missions you dropped from your log are left out of the profile, and so are the game's own helper quests (companion
  conversations, [DNT] ...).
- A mission whose reward window came up as it left your log is handed in as usual.
- A mission that came right after you used an item or in a holocom call: the bot waits a few seconds longer for it, and
  the Quest Recorder window says when you may have to do that part by hand.
- A mission whose title the game read a moment late is still picked up as a daily or weekly.
- Mission items kept in your bags are found too. Item names with an apostrophe no longer break the profile.
- Mission items used in a row: each use walks to its own spot and only takes an NPC close to where you used it. An item
  is never used on yourself when its NPC isn't there, an instant item is pressed once, and binoculars or scanners are no
  longer pressed again and again. After a fight or a death the row goes on where it was.
- A mission item you used right before a boss fight is used first, then the fight.
- Clicks for one task are no longer mixed with another task's clicks, and each count keeps to its own branch of the mission.
- One kill that counts for two tasks: the fight goes on until both are done and hunts both kinds of mobs.
- A count made by clicking several objects with the same name clicks each one once; only the last one waits for the count.
- A step where you only waited (for an NPC, a door, a timer): the bot waits there too, at most a little longer than you
  did, and goes on as soon as the step moves.
- Another mission's count that ticked during a fight but wasn't moved by it gets its own step.
- Daily, weekly and heroic missions are recognised in the German and French clients too. Heroic Transport and other items
  that move you are recognised in any client language.
- Missions from a mission board or terminal are taken only when the board lists them for you. If it doesn't (a weekly
  or daily you already did, or one not open to that character), the bot moves straight on instead of trying forever.
- A weekly already in your log is taken from the mission board you took it from. A weekly's "complete N missions" count no
  longer takes the last click or talk of the mission that just ended.
- Flying your ship to another planet and going through your ship's exit or another area's door are in the profile (check
  the flight in the Quest Recorder window).
- Dynamic encounters: the bot walks to where they start, like missions the game hands you by itself.
- A mission that starts right after a kill is not given by the object you clicked before the fight.
- Talking to your own companion is never a talk, pick-up or hand-in step.
- A revive at a medical center is no longer taken for a door, a shuttle or a mission item.
- Kills of your target are counted. A kill another player tagged first counts when your mission count moved with it.
- Non-English clients: mission items you used are matched from the cast bar and the combat log.
- Starting the bot no longer makes your own abilities look like quest abilities. Each cast of a quest ability counts.
- A quest log the game reads back empty for a moment no longer looks like every mission was dropped and picked up again.
- A holocom call that turns into a face-to-face talk (or the other way) is two conversations.
- In a group conversation, a reply another player won the roll for is not taken as yours.
- What you clicked before a loading screen is never matched to something after it.
- Quick teleports are seen even when the game was slow. An elevator window you closed without riding is not a ride.
- Logging in another character ends the recording and starts a new one for that character.
- Summer time ending or a clock correction no longer moves the times in a recording.
- If you change the profile while it records, your file is kept as you saved it and the recording goes on in
  "<name> (recorder copy).xml".
- Carrying on a recording works from the 3rd part on, only for the same character, only when the missions it left open are
  still in your log, and only where that run was. A weekly alone never carries a run on, and a profile you changed after
  the recording starts a new one.
- Make profile uses every part of a carried-on recording, and keeps your old profile in "_fixer backups" first.
- Files are written so a crash halfway leaves the old file as it was. A problem with the notes draft never stops the
  profile from being saved.
- Profile and recording folder settings with quotes or spaces around them work. A folder that can't be made falls back to
  the default one.
- The pause and wait hotkeys can be changed in the settings (Ctrl+Shift+P and Ctrl+Shift+W by default).
- Updates: your old version is backed up first and put back if the update fails halfway. Quest Recorder's folder is found
  even when it isn't called QuestRecorder. A slow check says "still checking" instead of "you have the latest".
- The installer saves and removes other copies of Quest Recorder in Plugins (like QuestRecorder-main), so BuddyCron loads
  only one.
- Record from here: the new bit goes in after the step you picked, with its fights and code, never after the profile's end.
  An unfinished bit isn't added.
- Control panel: fights get their own fight area, reward steps run while the reward waits, a weekly pick-up runs whenever
  it isn't in your log, and "Click once" walks there first. Steps you add get short labels.
- Control panel: it never saves over a profile that changed on disk (it loads it again), and makes no changes to the
  profile that is being recorded.
- "Do by hand" pop-ups from the review list and the control panel have a working Continue button.
- Send report: your character names and Windows user name are hidden without changing other words, it works on the
  recording going on now, and the profile of a carried-on recording is in it.

1.3
- Profiles you record now run on plain BuddyCron: anyone can load and run them without Quest Recorder installed.
  Every step is one of BuddyCron's own (PickUpQuest, TalkTo, UseObject, Interact, UseItem, UseInventoryItem, Grind, MoveTo,
  CompleteQuest). Mission items used in a row (runes) and special-bar quest abilities use BuddyCron's own code-in-profile
  (a Hook, like other community profiles), waiting for each cast before the next.
- Mission pickups are pinned to the exact giver you clicked, so a twin object with the same name is never picked
  (Black Hole: two "Black Hole Mission Terminal"s, only one has missions).
- A rune / item row fix: a corpse that finished the step is no longer lumped together with another task's corpse.
- Installed with git clone? BuddyCron's UpdateBuddy keeps it up to date, so Quest Recorder's own update pop-up stays out of the way.
- After every recording, a check warns if anything only Quest Recorder knows ended up in the profile.
- Missions the game hands you by itself (Section X [AREA] missions) no longer get the last enemy you targeted as their
  giver: the bot walks to where you got it instead (it went to Dreadtooth before).

1.2a
- Weeklies get picked up again on your 2nd and 3rd run of the week. The game calls a weekly "complete" after the
  first one, and the pick-up step took that as "already done". Weekly pick-ups now take it whenever the terminal
  offers it.
- "Do this part by hand, then press Continue" pop-ups now have a working Continue button.
- Mission items the combat log names by their cast bar text are matched too.

1.2
The profile now copies what you did, in your order.
- Steps come in the order you did them, each at the exact spot you used. BuddyCron finds the way in between
  (no walk points to stop at).
- Fights: each one at the spots where you fought it, only the mobs whose kill counted for that task. Nothing from
  elsewhere in the recording. Fights sit at the top of the profile (GrindAreas) and the steps use them by name.
- Clicks: only the exact object you clicked. When two of the same kind stand close together (CZ-198 pods), the
  bot stands where you stood first, so it takes the same one you did.
- Selecting an object and attacking it counts as using that object.
- A lift you opened but walked past: the bot walks past it too instead of getting stuck at it.
- A count you stopped short of only runs on to the full count for plain fights (no waiting forever at a used pod).
- A new recording never joins onto a profile you already saved from the review list.
- A task's last count is written as its count (35 of 35), not just "step done".
- Containers that respawn in the same spot count as new ones, so every click you made is in the profile.
- Casts are recorded from the moment they start, so a step's cause comes before its tick, and a cast isn't lost
  when a fight starts. A loot that finishes just after the tick stays with that step.
- Your target is recorded every time it changes, and looting a dead mob is a loot, not "talked to an NPC".
- Quests that finish by themselves aren't "handed in" to whatever you clicked last.
- Lifts are left to BuddyCron, which rides them itself.
- The profile ends with "Done" (or tells you what isn't finished) instead of starting itself over.
- Distances fixed everywhere: game positions are in 10 m units, and about 20 checks treated them as metres.

- Mission items, exactly as you used them: where you stood, what you used it on (yourself, an NPC by name, a
  spot), your exact distance (the bot uses it from there - no more "TooFar"), and every use in your order: the last
  one, items with no cast bar, stims and injectors. Medpacs and items the game takes off you are left out. If the
  recorder can't tell, the step is yellow - it never guesses or drops a use.
- An item used on a corpse is used on dead ones (DeadOnly). An item used in a fight on a weakened mob: the bot
  fights it first and uses the item once its health is down to where you did.
- Several items in a row (runes): each one in your order, on the NPC you used it on, from as close as you stood.
- Scanner: while you record, the recorder asks the game what each mission counts and saves every one of those
  objects and mobs near you, also the ones you only walked past. Only what your missions still need.
- Collect steps (a counter fed by eggs, bones, and loot): one step until it's full. It takes any of those objects
  within 150 m, with one spot per cluster so the bot's loop is short.
- Fights also get the spots of linked groups of the same mobs near where you fought (within ~150 m), so the bot
  moves on to the next group instead of waiting for a respawn.
- Rewards: the reward is taken when the game says it's waiting, even if the quest already counts as done.
- Quest abilities from a special / temporary bar (Macrobinoculars, the Seeker Droid): recorded like mission items
  (where, on whom, how close, in your order) and cast by the new UseQuestAbility tag, which opens the bar with its
  mission item first. Only kept when a mission moved right after, so a new class ability never becomes a step.
- Putting on a quest item (a disguise): its own step (EquipItem), right before the step it was for. A gear swap
  with no mission progress after it is left out.
- Defend / hold a spot: when you held a spot while mobs came at you and the step moved on by itself, the bot waits
  there, fighting, until it moves on (instead of walking there and leaving).
- Mission items the game gives no sign for (no cast bar, no cooldown, nothing leaves your bags - the Serum Injector):
  the recorder also reads the game's own combat log, which names the item and who you used it on. Turn on
  "Enable Combat Logging" in the game's Preferences (Combat Logging) for this.
- A loot with no corpse click right after a kill (the game's area loot) counts as looting that mob, and a step
  finished by looting a mob you killed earlier becomes "kill and loot" it.
- An item the game takes when a mission finishes (handed in) is never guessed as used on someone.
- Clicks on side-by-side objects go by the exact spot you clicked, in your order (new ClickObject tag).
- A quick travel or fleet pass you cast never becomes walk points.
- Picking up a quest waits for the quest board to open. The summary waits for the save. The review list won't
  save over a profile that was changed outside it.

1.1
Recording - everything you do, nothing guessed:
- "What you did (live)": every click, item, reply, window, board and lift shows up as you play, with what it did
  in your quest log. OK = the bot does it by itself. !! = it can't, the line says why, and it pops up in game.
- Each recording also saves "(what you did).txt": every action, exactly where, and what changed in your quest log.
- The quest giver is whatever you clicked right before it landed in your log (General Konya for End of Torvix, the
  Recon Droid for Asset Liquidation, the terminal for the dailies).
- Steps are the clicks you made, in your order, at the exact spots - no more guessing objects from their names.
- The interact key counts as a use, and every key or mouse action over an object is recorded.
- Conversations: the reply you pick is recorded. Light or dark picks use TempConvoSkip; other picks use the new
  TalkAndReply step, which picks the same wheel numbers you did.
- Mission items: caught by cast, cooldown, a stack being used up, quest items in your bags, and "Using X..." casts.
  Several used one after another (the Sith runes) are each used once, in your order.
- Scanner: when you click a quest object, every other one with the same name close by is noted too.
- Hotkeys while recording: Ctrl+Shift+P = pause here (you do it by hand), Ctrl+Shift+W = wait here 10 s,
  Ctrl+Shift+Z = ignore your last action.

After you stop - you're in charge:
- Review list: your profile step by step in plain words. Untick a step to leave it out, move it up or down, or
  mark it "do by hand", then Save. No XML. Steps with no proof (nothing you did was seen) are yellow.

Better profiles:
- Fights roam every spot you killed at; one kill counting for two quests keeps both fights going; a task you
  stopped short of still runs to the full count.
- "Click any of these" is one step with every spot, so the bot uses whichever one is there.
- Puzzles (6+ clicks on the same object in one step, like the End of Torvix pipeline controls) become a
  "do it by hand" pause on that exact object.
- The thing you clicked right before a boss fight (Kregg's Locker) is clicked again first.
- Pick-ups give up after 3 tries if the giver isn't offering the quest (no more clicking a terminal forever), and
  handle mission boards and conversations by themselves. An NPC's own quest window (the Recon Droid) still needs
  you to press Accept - the profile asks you.
- Heroic shuttles: taken from the Fleet too, and when you started recording mid-cast.
